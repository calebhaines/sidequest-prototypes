# Lady Supine — ten art directions

[Open the comparison gallery](https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/lady-supine/style-studies/) for side-by-side comparisons of whole sheets or matching subjects, full-size viewing, original PNG downloads, and a saved shortlist.

These are exploratory asset sheets for **Lady Supine: Entering the Unenterable Entrance**. They use the same subjects to compare ten approaches to the game's bleak, handmade world. **Direction 03, Torn paper, has been selected and implemented in the playable game.** The other nine remain alternatives for comparison.

Every sheet has six panels, read left to right:

| Row | First panel | Second panel | Third panel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Top | Lady Supine, the beautiful human ruler with an immense open-topped head | Ada, a tired hare friend, and an Aristobat | Shanty Hours: a patched trailer and Supine's billboard |
| Bottom | Hospital interior and improvised brain-exchange equipment | Library of Time Well Spent interior | Curly willow, poor lakeshore, lantern, fishing float, and time coupon |

The studies use the game's 24 base pigments as their color direction. Paper, ink, cloth, and paint textures introduce natural blends. The original generated PNGs are preserved without resizing or pixel-art conversion.

## Sheets

1. [Heavy marker](01-heavy-marker.png) — dragged contours, dirty fills, and restless scratches.
2. [Drypoint and ink](02-drypoint-ink.png) — wiry incisions, rubbed plate tone, and restrained color.
3. [Torn paper](03-torn-paper.png) — flat fragments, ragged edges, and visible pasted repairs.
4. [Geometric gouache](04-geometric-gouache.png) — opaque crooked planes and dry brush texture.
5. [Stained watercolor](05-stained-watercolor.png) — damp washes, tide marks, and thin ink.
6. [Linocut](06-linocut.png) — heavy silhouettes and rough carved negative lines.
7. [Oil pastel](07-oil-pastel.png) — dense grain, rubbed wax, and scratched layers.
8. [Stitched patchwork](08-stitched-patchwork.png) — frayed institutional cloth and strained seams.
9. [Puppet theatre](09-puppet-theatre.png) — painted cardboard, wire joints, and shallow stage depth.
10. [Misregistered print](10-misregistered-print.png) — offset ink layers and degraded civic forms.
