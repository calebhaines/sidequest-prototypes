ROTATION STATION / OPERATING NOTES

Load. Rotate. Repeat.

ROTISSERIE mixes four independent stereo loops. Every deck has its own region, speed, direction, tone, tape character, and position. Select a deck to edit it; changing its controls does not stop the other loops.

Make a loop

Choose a house recipe and press Play, or import audio into a deck. Trim Region start and Region end to choose what repeats. Speed changes pitch and length. Tempo match adjusts playback to the project tempo by fitting the selected region to Loop beats at the project tempo.

Record & overdub

Choose Built-in keys, Microphone, Keys + microphone, or Other decks + keys. Other decks records the rest of the mix and the playable keys, excluding the selected deck. Choose a length in bars and press Record loop. With Start on next bar enabled, recording waits for the next bar while playing. Overdub adds to the selected loop while following its current speed and direction. Overdub feedback controls how much of the previous layer survives each pass. Stop finishes the current take; Panic cancels it. Undo restores the previous take.

Play the instrument

Click or touch the keyboard, or use A W S E D F T G Y H U J K. Choose a voice and shape its envelope. The instrument feeds the output and can be recorded directly without a microphone.

Keep your sounds

Save project includes all loop audio and settings. Open project restores them. Export WAV renders the mix or a single deck. Record output captures a live performance, including gestures and the playable instrument. Each imported or recorded loop can contain up to 30 seconds; output recording can run for 3 minutes.
