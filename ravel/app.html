RAVEL is a stereo sample slicer. Each of its sixteen steps can play a different slice, with its own velocity, probability, pitch, direction, ratchets, gate and timing.

Make a commotion

Press Play to hear a study. Tap the sixteen pads to audition slices. Select a sequencer step, then tap a pad or use the Slice menu to assign it. The small switch on each step enables or disables it.

Slice evenly cuts the trimmed ribbon into sixteen pieces. Find transients follows attacks in the audio. Drag a waveform line to move a boundary. Use Trim start/end to change the ribbon’s overall region.

Stop pauses the sequencer; the pads remain playable. Panic cancels pending sound and clears effect tails. Patterns A–D switch on the next bar. Chain advances through all four. Shuffle rearranges slice assignments; Mischief changes a few expressive decisions. Undo restores edits, imports and microphone takes.

Bring your own sound

Import an audio file under 20 MB; RAVEL keeps the first 20 seconds and preserves stereo. Microphone recording explicitly requests permission, stops after 20 seconds, and is never monitored to speakers. Tap it again to finish. Panic discards a pending take and releases the microphone.

Keep the pieces

Save/Open project includes the complete sample and all four patterns. Local recovery saves when browser storage is available. Render WAV produces 1–16 bars with a three-second effect tail. Capture live mix records the actual output for up to three minutes; tap again to download.

Keyboard

Space Play / Stop · Esc Panic · Ctrl+Z Undo.
The pad keys are 1 2 3 4, Q W E R, A S D F, Z X C V. Shortcuts pause while typing.
