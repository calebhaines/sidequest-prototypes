KITCHEN / OPERATING NOTES

Give every note
something to say.

Choose Alto, Tenor, Bass or Soprano. Type a short English phrase and press Distribute words. The words spread across your melody. Press Play phrase to hear them.

Select a numbered step to edit its word, note, voice and velocity. Note length belongs to that note: mix short, dotted and sustained notes in the same phrase, or enter a custom number of beats. One beat is a quarter note. Drag the right edge of the small timing bar to resize a note; arrow keys also work. Spacing only sets the distance between step starts. Notes may overlap, and they end at the phrase boundary. Auto / fit gap keeps the original automatic length and its articulation control.

Word to play is for the keyboard. Try a word, a short phrase, or a sound such as ah, ee, oo or mm. Click the keys or use A–K. The first new word may need a moment to prepare.

The five knobs shape Tone, Air, Vibrato, Space and Volume. Drag a knob up or down; use the arrow keys for small changes. Double-click restores its starting value.

Save recipe keeps the complete voice, phrase and melody. WAV exports can be used in any sampler. The shared Samples and Patterns panels move sounds and editable phrases between Kitchen instruments.

This is a synthetic English voice instrument. Everything runs in your browser, including saved audio. No account or microphone is needed.
