THE REFERENCE DESK

A little room to play.

Explore the octave. Sing a note, play it back, and notice the space between pitches.

Relative pitch is a skill. This lab asks how notes relate to each other. You do not need to name a note from silence.

Make it repeatable. Try a ten-question set at the same level. Sing the answer before checking, and revisit sounds you confuse.

Listen before looking. Answers stay hidden until you commit or reveal. In practice, replaying never lowers your score; a reveal is logged as a skip. Adaptive pitch tests use one completed listen per trial and show feedback at the end.
