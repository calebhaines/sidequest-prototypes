Make your first arrangement

Press Play to hear the prepared session. Select a track, choose one of eleven bundled Kitchen instruments, and use its full editor. Arm the track with its round button, then Record to capture its output as an editable audio clip. Pause finishes the take and holds its position; Stop finishes it and returns to the beginning. The recording button can also finish a take and hold the position. Panic discards it.

Import audio directly into the selected track. Microphone recording asks for permission and records into the selected track. Open Microphone & interface · monitoring & timing to enable live input through that track’s four effects and mixer, even while stopped. Monitoring starts off; use headphones to prevent feedback. Changing the selected track does not silently move the input: use Move monitoring here. Pause, Stop, Panic, and opening a project turn monitoring off. The recording button finishes a take while retaining monitoring when enabled. Recording continues across transport loops. The session extends to keep the complete take, up to 64 bars; a take stops after two minutes or at the 64-bar capacity, whichever comes first. Longer imported sources remain available through the clip’s source controls.

Before service

Count-in plays one or two bars of clicks before a new take starts, without recording the clicks. Punch in / out records only between the chosen boundaries while the arrangement plays around the take. Use the loop range as a quick starting point. Punch recording makes one pass through its range; an ordinary take can continue through transport loops.

Microphone timing and BROILER

Recording alignment compensates microphone takes against the arrangement. Auto estimates the input, output, and processing delay reported by your browser, then applies the extra trim. Manual uses only your signed offset; Off leaves timing unchanged. Positive offsets move the take earlier and negative offsets move it later. Settings lock during a take. Auto is an estimate, so fine-tune it by ear for your interface. Compensation keeps take lengths and punch boundaries intact; it does not remove the delay you hear while monitoring.

BROILER is the bass-first amp rack: bass and guitar amp characters, including American valve classics, clean low-end blending, tone shaping, power-stage sag, speaker drive, eleven cabinet choices including direct, microphone position, and a gate. Choose a recipe preset or shape each stage yourself. Use it on recorded clips, instrument tracks, or the monitored microphone.

GLAZE · the vocal finishing station

GLAZE combines cleanup, de-essing, compression, vocal EQ, saturation, transpose and scale correction, fixed-interval harmonies, doubling, vowel resonators, a twelve-band vocoder, ring modulation, rhythmic chopping, ducked delay and reverb, and a peak guard. Choose a house recipe, use Essentials for the main controls, or open Full pantry for every setting. Each stage has its own switch. Live readings show the selected insert’s actual processing when audio is running.

Use Sing through GLAZE in the microphone panel or Voice → GLAZE in Audio settings to connect the selected track without replacing occupied effects. Pitch and harmony add a rolling-window delay; scale correction also needs time to track a new monophonic note. Keep them off for the quickest live response. Vowel shift colours resonant filters, and harmony intervals are fixed. Recorded microphone takes remain dry so you can change the strip afterwards.

SCALES · tune, measure, tidy

SCALES is the utility insert: a single-note tuner with chromatic, guitar, drop-D, bass, five-string bass, and ukulele guides; adjustable A4 calibration; sample peak and RMS meters; correlation; peak holds and clip flags; trims; channel routing; polarity; width; mono bass; DC removal and cleanup filters; a final peak guard; and output mute. Default settings preserve the incoming audio. Choose a string to hold its target, or Auto to follow the nearest note or guide string. The tuner measures audio entering this insert. Move SCALES before distorted effects for the clearest reading.

Use Input → SCALES in Audio settings to connect your selected interface without replacing any occupied effect. Output mute allows quiet tuning while analysis continues. Bypass skips all processing and analysis, including mute. Reset peak holds & clips clears the meter history without changing sound settings. The tuner and meters add no audio delay; a stable low note still needs time to be measured.

BATTER · acoustic drums and moving ingredients

BATTER now includes 148 recorded samples across four offline starter kits. Each of its twelve drum lanes can blend an A and B sample source; both can use recorded velocity layers and alternate hits. Open the blend controls to choose the second source and balance the two sounds. The lane LFO adds repeating motion to pitch, filter tone, pan, level, or that blend, using musical divisions or a free rate. Saved projects and printed audio retain both sources and modulation.

Clear pattern empties all hits in the selected BATTER bank, including steps on other pages and protected hits. Other banks and sample settings stay in place. Undo restores the complete pattern.

Move the playhead

Play starts or continues from the current position. Pause holds the position; Stop returns to the beginning. The lower transport row moves to the start or end, steps backward or forward by one bar, and restarts playback from the beginning. Click the clock to enter an exact bar, beat, and tick, click or drag the ruler to seek, or drag the needle’s handle. Seeking also moves playing audio and waits while recording is preparing, active, or finalizing.

Jump directly to Loop start or Loop end from the transport. Set the range with the numeric loop boundaries, or choose Loop this clip in the clip inspector. Follow keeps the moving needle in view; switch it off to explore another part of the arrangement. With Loop off, playback stops at the end of the session.

Arrange the ingredients

Drag a clip to move it in time or to another track. Drag its edges to trim. The clip inspector offers precise timing, source boundaries, speed/pitch, reverse, loop, level, and fades. Split at the playhead or duplicate to build a phrase. Use Snap and Zoom to set the detail you need.

Sections and clip actions

Add named, colored Markers at the playhead, then click a section to jump there. Use its pencil button to rename, move, or delete it. Select a recording and use Delete clip directly below the timeline; Undo restores it. Right-click a clip for its actions. Send to instrument transfers its edited audio into a new or existing STOCK sample zone, HOTPLATE layer, REDUCE source, ROTISSERIE deck, DICER sample, STEAM spectral score, or BATTER drum lane. Choose an empty track to load the recipient, or an existing matching instrument. Replacing an existing sample requires the replacement checkbox.

A shared sample pantry

Samples opens a local library shared with the other Kitchen apps. Render an instrument or clip into the library, choose a sample to receive, and trim it to its destination’s limit. Portable sample packets preserve audio, tempo, and its source; WAV files work with other music software. The library uses this browser’s storage; save or download audio you want to keep. Samples received in GALLEY become arranged audio clips.

The editable recipe

+ Note clip makes an editable musical phrase on the selected track. Choose its instrument, then open Edit notes: click the grid to add notes, drag to move, and pull a note’s right edge to change its duration. Shift-click selects more notes; the selection tool draws a box. Exact note, beat, duration, and velocity controls are below the grid. Arrow keys move selected notes; Shift + left/right changes their length. Delete removes selected notes, and Ctrl/Cmd + A selects all notes when the piano roll has focus.

Patterns shares notes and rhythms between apps as editable musical parts. Drum patterns include named voices; map those voices explicitly when receiving a rhythm in another instrument. Notes on a GALLEY track play through its instrument and four effects. Print audio captures the phrase while keeping its patch and pattern attached to the resulting clip.

Printed audio with the recipe attached

Printed clips remember the instrument, settings, and pattern that made them. Edit source reopens that saved instrument. Change the sound or edit source notes in the piano roll. After changing its native sequencer, choose Use instrument pattern to copy that part into the source. Then choose Update audio to replace the clip’s source while keeping its arrangement, trims, level, fades, and effects. The saved source lives in your GALLEY project; Undo restores earlier audio.

One clock for the whole kitchen

Compatible instruments follow GALLEY’s tempo, bar and beat, seeks, and loop boundaries. Live patterns line up with the arrangement after a seek or loop, and queued pattern changes can land on the next bar. Note clips use their clip position as the musical clock.

Automate the controls

Select a track and an automation target, then add a lane. Click the curve to add a point and drag points to change their time or value; precise point controls work with a keyboard too. Choose Linear ramps or Hold steps. Switch a lane off to hear the manual setting. Arm Write while playback runs and move mixer or effect controls to record their movement. Write disarms when you switch tracks. Automation is saved in projects and included in WAV exports.

Mix and shape

Every track has level, pan, mute, solo, and exactly four effect slots. Select a slot to edit it; add any of the eleven effects, bypass, reset, remove, or change its order. Effect order changes the sound. Live mode starts a loaded instrument alongside the DAW transport; turn it off when playing its recorded clips to avoid doubling.

Keep your work

Save/Open project includes audio, instrument settings, inserted effects, and uploaded instrument HTML. Undo/Redo restores arrangement, mixer, and effect edits. Browser recovery is optional when local storage is available. Stereo WAV export renders your arranged clips; you can export the master mix or individual stems, with an optional effect tail. Save a project before closing the browser.

Keys and accessibility

Space Play/Pause · Enter Stop and return to the beginning · Shift Space Play from the beginning · Home/End session start/end · Alt ←/Alt → move one bar · M Add marker · G Go to position · [/] jump to loop start/end · F Follow · L Loop · Shift L Loop selected clip · Escape Panic when no dialog is open · Ctrl/Cmd Z Undo · Ctrl/Cmd Shift Z Redo · Delete selected clip · D Duplicate · S Split. On a focused clip, arrow keys move by the snap interval, and Shift + arrow changes its length. Numeric controls are available for every clip edit.

Future instruments

Upload a standalone HTML Web Audio instrument, or load a same-origin URL. Existing Kitchen apps use an audio bridge to route their output through the selected track. Future apps can implement the documented Kitchen host interface for transport and project state. Untrusted instrument HTML should only be loaded when you trust its author.

GALLEY uses eight tracks and four insert slots per track. Audio and note clips share the same arrangement. Printed clips retain their musical source, and compatible instruments follow the studio clock. Large projects consume browser memory. No audio is uploaded.
