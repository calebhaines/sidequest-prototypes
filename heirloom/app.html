HEIRLOOM / OPERATING NOTES

Some things deserve to be passed down.

Start with a sound

Choose a factory recipe and play the keyboard. HEIRLOOM has five voices, each with two oscillators, a resonant low-pass filter and separate filter and amplifier envelopes. Its signal is mono, written equally to both channels.

Turn the ingredients

Drag a knob vertically. Hold Shift for fine adjustment. Arrow keys move one step; Shift + arrows move ten. Home and End select the limits, and double-click, Enter or Space restores the default. Type an exact value beneath any knob. Each complete gesture is one Undo.

Oscillators and modulation

Waveform switches are independent, so several may be on together. Sync resets oscillator A from oscillator B. Oscillator B can ignore keyboard pitch or run at low frequency, and its Poly-mod signal is available even when B is turned down in the mixer.

Poly-mod sends the filter envelope and oscillator B to A frequency, A pulse width and filter frequency. Wheel modulation blends LFO and noise and sends them to the destinations shown. Raise the modulation wheel to hear more movement. The LFO waveform switches combine too.

Shape and compare

Attack, Decay, Sustain and Release define the filter and amplifier shapes. Filter resonance can sing on its own. Keyboard tracking and circuit character controls help the sound follow the range you play. Open Inside the cabinet for calibration and useful extensions.

A / B compares two sound snapshots while retaining your notes and tempo. Copy transfers the current sound to the other slot. Save recipe writes the current sound and phrase; Undo brings back earlier edits.

Play or write

The on-screen and computer keyboards play directly. Connect MIDI for notes, velocity, pitch bend, modulation wheel and sustain. Z and X change the keyboard octave. Space starts or stops the phrase; Escape silences all voices.

Open The phrase to draw polyphonic notes. Drag notes to move them; drag the right edge to change length. Select a note for exact start, length, pitch and velocity. Delete removes the selection; arrow keys move it, and Shift moves by an octave or a beat.

Keep it

Save/Open retains the complete recipe and phrase. WAV exports the actual instrument. Browser recovery is available when storage permits. The sound models follow the original architecture, with documented software approximations and optional calibration controls.
