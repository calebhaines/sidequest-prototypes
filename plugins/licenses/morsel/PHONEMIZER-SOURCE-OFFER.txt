MORSEL eSpeak NG phonemizer source access

The complete preferred upstream phonemizer.js JavaScript sources, packed data,
configuration and license accompany this download in phonemizer-source.zip.
They are pinned to xenova/phonemizer.js commit
6835144b7ee9043129222549c1ed2f6a27216278.

The underlying eSpeak NG is GPLv3 (see ../COPYING). Full upstream eSpeak NG
1.52.0 C/C++, pronunciation dictionaries, phoneme sources, Emscripten glue and
build scripts are available without charge from the same Kitchen download site:

https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/music/morsel/MORSEL-phonemizer-C-source.zip

Supplement SHA-256:
b4517592e3cbc43703bb1c782702eafb98097659e0b7e96e69c32ee32ae5003a

Upstream archive:
https://codeload.github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/zip/refs/tags/1.52.0

The phonemizer package does not identify its historical C/compiler revision;
we do not claim a modern C rebuild produces identical compiled JavaScript.
The exact unmodified JavaScript used by MORSEL is included, and no recompilation
or source-supplement download is needed to rebuild the standalone HTML offline.
