WELCOME TO THE NEIGHBORHOOD

A picnic for your ears.

Each of the six parts has a sound, a row in the pattern book, and its own place in the mix. Tap a step to add or remove it. Shift-click a step to inspect it without changing it. The inspector accepts up to six notes, including names like C4 and G4.

Patterns A–D keep separate 16- or 32-step phrases. Use the page buttons to reach steps 17–32. Play A → D makes a four-pattern walk. Grow this pattern writes a new original phrase using your key, chords, hour and weather; Undo brings the old notes back.

The sound cupboard searches the complete collection. Choose a part, then a sound. ♫ loads and auditions a sound. The piano plays the selected part; the letter keys shown above it work too. Mute and Solo affect playback and exports.

Space plays or stops; Escape silences everything; Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes; Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z redoes. Save a project for a portable copy. Patterns shares notes; Samples shares rendered audio.
