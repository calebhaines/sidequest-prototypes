Ten more ways to assemble
a world running out.

Eight new torn-paper concepts change what the world is built from: office cast-offs, laundry lines, abandoned buses, and other exhausted things. Two more imagine clipped photographic collages. Compare them with the torn paper now in the game, or revisit the original ten directions.

The shared brief

A bleak, jury-rigged trailer park under a beautiful and terrifying ruler. Things have been repaired too often and replaced too rarely. Lady Supine is human, with an enormous head and a hole in the top of her head. Ada and the other residents are part beast. The new studies vary the shapes, construction, clothing, and equipment while keeping that world intact.

Concepts 11–18 use the game's torn-paper direction and 24 base pigments. Concepts 19–20 use generated photographic fragments arranged as clipped stock-image collages. All ten new sheets are for comparison; direction 03 is currently implemented in the game.
