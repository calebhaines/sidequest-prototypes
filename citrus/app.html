CITRUS / OPERATING NOTES

A little acid goes a long way.

Start with the filter

Choose Saw or Square, press Play, then turn Cutoff, Resonance and Envelope modulation. The single oscillator feeds a nonlinear diode ladder and a coupled filter / amplifier contour. Decay changes the normal filter envelope; Accent changes the sound’s emphasis and contour when a step is accented.

Seven knobs, no guessing

Drag a knob vertically; hold Shift for fine adjustment. Arrow keys change its value, Page Up / Down makes larger moves, and Home / End selects the limits. Enter, Space or a double-click restores its default. Every knob has an exact numeric field. A whole gesture is one Undo.

Write the bass line

Select bank A–H, then click a step to inspect its pitch, Note, Accent and Slide. A step without Note is a rest. Double-click or Shift-click a step to switch note / rest quickly. The selected-step buttons and exact MIDI pitch field work with a keyboard. Arrow keys move between steps; Delete makes a rest.

Slide belongs to the outgoing step: it joins that note’s gate to the next sounding step and moves its pitch without a fresh attack. A rest breaks the connection. Consecutive accents retain the circuit’s characteristic interaction rather than acting as a generic velocity knob.

Let a few patterns develop

Use Chain to repeat a short order of banks. Add the selected bank, select a chain entry, then choose its bank and repeats. Arrows change the order; Remove takes one entry out. Copy → next duplicates the current bank into its neighbor; Undo restores the previous pattern. Clear empties only the selected bank.

Play by hand

The keyboard and MIDI input use one voice with held-note priority. Computer keys A W S E D F… play notes; Z / X changes octave. Write keys → step writes each played pitch into the selected step and advances to the next one. With it off, the keyboard auditions without changing the pattern.

Keep the original timing

Patterns imports can retain an exact phrase that does not fit the sixteen-step grid. A banner identifies that phrase. Use step patterns, or edit a native step, to return to the step sequencer; Undo restores the imported phrase. GALLEY’s piano roll and printed sources retain the exact notes and articulation.

Inside the peel

Slide time, filter drive and optional drift are useful departures from the stock circuit. High quality uses four-times oversampling; Eco uses twice. Both use the same synthesis model. The model follows the original architecture, with documented component and calibration approximations.

Save and share

Save recipe keeps the sound, eight banks, chain and any imported phrase. Samples shares rendered sound; Patterns shares editable notes. WAV export renders up to 64 bars, subject to the instrument’s duration limit. A single-note export makes a useful bass sample. Browser recovery is used when available; keep a portable project before closing.
