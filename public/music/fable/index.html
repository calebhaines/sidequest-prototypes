FABLE / FIELD NOTES

A cupboard that plays back.

Collect a sound. Give it a key, a temperament, and perhaps a small misunderstanding.

Build an instrument

Import audio or receive a sample from the shared Samples library. Dropping files onto a keyboard key or the zone map assigns consecutive keys; ordinary imports estimate root pitches. Each zone pairs one sample with a key range and velocity range. Several zones can reuse the same sample. Save instrument downloads a complete project, including imported audio.

Shape the sample

Drag trim and loop handles on the waveform, or enter normalized positions from 0 to 1. Find root note estimates pitch; set Root note manually for difficult or percussive sounds. Gate follows note releases; One-shot plays through. Classic changes pitch by playback rate. Granular texture separates pitch from sample scanning speed.

Map and layer

Keys distributes zones across the keyboard. Velocity creates soft-to-hard layers; Drums assigns consecutive keys. Round-robin alternates matching zones in a numbered group. Choke stops previous notes in the same group. Split to zones slices the selected sample into consecutive playable regions.

Play and modulate

Use the screen keyboard, computer keys, or a connected MIDI device. Sustain holds notes until released. MIDI supports velocity, sustain, pitch bend, modulation wheel, and pressure. Envelopes shape level and filter; envelope time entries are in seconds. Two LFOs and eight modulation routes animate pitch, cutoff, pan, level, start, and texture position.

Sequence and export

Four patterns contain 16, 32, or 64 steps. Click a step to toggle it and edit its note, velocity, gate, probability, or ratchets. Performance mode selects the sequencer or held-note arpeggiator. Export audio renders a sample zone or pattern through the instrument’s effects, or downloads one unprocessed sample as WAV and every sample together in a ZIP. The Samples panel saves audio into your local library for other Music Lab apps.

Keep your collection

Projects and sample libraries are stored locally in this browser when storage is available. Downloads travel between browsers and devices. Stop halts the clock; Panic immediately clears notes and effects. Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record.
