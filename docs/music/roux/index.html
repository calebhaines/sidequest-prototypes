ROUX / OPERATING NOTES

Keep the bottom moving.

ROUX is a monophonic bass synthesizer with a circular, relative-note recipe. A deep sine fundamental blends with a harmonic oscillator and a tuned waveguide, then passes through drive, a resonant filter, envelopes, and stereo echo.

Stir the recipe

Select any numbered stop around the pot. Move adds scale degrees to the previous pitch; Root returns to the root note; Hold joins the previous note; Rest leaves space. Octave offsets, velocity, probability, gate, accent, and slide belong to each stop. The note shown is the result of the first circuit. Drift transposes each later circuit by scale degrees; the complete four-turn phrase repeats. Probability is reproducible from the saved seed.

Length chooses the active stops. Stride skips through the ring; Direction reverses or bounces traversal. Rotation changes the starting stop. Drift changes the pitch after each complete circuit. Swing offsets alternate steps. Fresh recipe builds a seeded, playable bass line while keeping the sound patch.

Independent cycles

Accent and slide each have their own Euclidean pulse cycle. Length, Pulses, and Rotation determine when each cycle fires. They combine with per-stop Accent and Slide. Different cycle lengths create longer rhythmic phrases without requiring a longer recipe. Accent amount controls the strength of its extra emphasis.

Share the order

Samples saves rendered audio to the shared pantry. Patterns stores exact pitched notes for other instruments or GALLEY. Imported patterns remain exact and are saved with the recipe. Use native recipe returns to circular sequencing; changing native recipe controls also returns to native sequencing.

Build the bass

Oscillator supplies upper harmonics; Sub supplies a deep fundamental. The vessel is a tuned feedback delay with damping, excitation, bite, and decay. Copper, iron, ceramic, and rubber alter excitation and resonance. Body tuning shifts its pitch relative to the oscillator; extreme settings are deliberately less orderly.

Amplitude and filter have separate ADSR envelopes. Filter-envelope amount is measured in octaves. Drive and Fold shape the mixed signal before the resonant filter. Pitch envelope adds a brief note-start bend. Glide sets the time between sliding notes; Legato preserves the envelope across a slide. The LFO can modulate cutoff, body, pitch, drive, or pulse width, freely or in sync.

Keep a copy

Save recipe downloads the complete sound and sequence. Open restores a ROUX project. Undo and Redo restore edits. Export WAV renders the recipe without changing the live transport. Stop ends new sequenced notes; Panic clears sound. Return to beginning resets the sequence and restarts if it was playing.

Keyboard & MIDI

A S D F G H J K audition scale notes. Space plays or stops; Escape clears sound. Ctrl / Command Z undoes; Ctrl / Command Shift Z redoes. Connect MIDI requests access to attached MIDI devices where your browser supports it. Note On plays notes; CC 74 controls filter cutoff. No microphone permission is requested.

Everything runs in this browser. Audio begins after a click or key press. Local storage keeps your current recipe when available; downloaded projects and standalone HTML remain portable.
