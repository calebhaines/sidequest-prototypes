The shared brief

A bleak, patched trailer park under a beautiful and terrifying ruler. Things have been repaired too often and replaced too rarely. Lady Supine is human, with an enormous head and a hole in the top of her head. These studies explore abstract, handmade art with the game's 24 pigment colors as their palette direction.
