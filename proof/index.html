LEAVEN / Operating notes

Choose a factory recipe, then press Play. The eight chords cycle in order; select a slot to change its ingredients. Enable unused slots in the chord inspector.

Select any of the sixteen steps to edit its velocity, gate, chance, repeats, timing, or octave. The On button switches a step off. Tie carries the previous note; Protect keeps a step safe from mutation.

For your own chord, choose “Keyboard / held notes”. Play the keys or connect MIDI. Latch keeps the chord after you release it. A fresh chord replaces the last one when all keys have been released.

Space plays or stops. Escape silences everything. Home restarts. Z / X shifts the keyboard. Ctrl / ⌘ Z undoes; add Shift to redo. Hold “Fill” for temporary double hits.

WAV prints the actual instrument in stereo. MIDI saves the realized notes and timing. Save project keeps the complete recipe. Browser recovery works when storage is available.

Every sound section stays on the front panel. Model character controls become available when their engine is selected. Sound begins only after you press a playback control or key.
