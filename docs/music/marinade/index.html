MARINADE / Operating notes

Start with a factory recipe, play a key, and move the central blend slider. Ingredient A and ingredient B are analysed into changing harmonic spectra, then rebuilt as a tuned, polyphonic sound.

Load your own audio into either bay or drag it onto the vessel. Use trim to choose a useful region, Root note to tune it, and Freeze position to select the moment you want to play. Analysis combines stereo into mono, while the final instrument plays in stereo. Save project embeds your imported ingredients.

Draw the morph path from left to right: the top favours ingredient B, the bottom ingredient A. Enable movement and choose its duration to make the blend evolve with every note. The independent pitch, texture, transient, and formant controls change what each ingredient contributes.

The sixteen-step pattern plays pitched notes. Select a step to edit it; double-click to switch it on or off. Clear pattern and Season notes support Undo, and protected steps survive seasoning. Each step can hold a single note or a chord.

Space starts or stops, Escape silences everything, Z / X moves the keyboard, and Ctrl / ⌘ Z undoes. Connect MIDI to play an attached keyboard. WAV exports use the same synthesis engine as playback.

Source samples and rendered sounds connect to Kitchen’s shared Samples library; editable notes travel through Patterns. Sound begins only after you press a playback control or key.
