A GUIDE TO THE RACKET

The hiss has rules.

Blend drum bodies and noise, shape their envelopes, and set the sound in motion. The hiss has brought its own teeth.

Build your beat

Click a step to add a hit. Shift + click for a louder accent, or press and hold on a touchscreen. Select a voice to blend its BODY and NOISE layers, level, and pan. Open Synthesis to edit the drum oscillators, separate envelopes, noise filter, distortion, and modulation. Modulation restarts on each hit; tempo sync sets its rate from the beat. Drag knobs up or down; hold Shift for fine adjustments. Use M to mute a track or S to hear it solo.

Make it yours

Try a curated preset, or randomize for a new direction. Pattern banks A–D hold four variations. Copy sends your current pattern to the next bank. Changes are saved in this browser automatically. Older projects upgrade to the new synthesis controls when opened.

Take it with you

Save project downloads an editable JSON file. The folder button opens it again. Export WAV renders four bars plus an effect tail as a stereo audio file, ready for your DAW.
