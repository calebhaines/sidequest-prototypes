## Overtone — musical ear training

[**Open Overtone**](https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/ear-training/) for a dark, terminal-inspired listening lab: interval identification, chord quality, chord progressions, exact melody reconstruction, scale identification, rhythm reconstruction, relative pitch discrimination, and simultaneous harmony + melody reconstruction. Each module has **eight graduated levels**, a concrete description of the current settings, ten-question practice sets, unlimited practice replays, answer explanations, and keyboard controls. The field guide explains every level. Melodies grow from two to nine notes; rhythm levels 1–5 use eighth notes and levels 6–8 use sixteenths. Pitch practice narrows the gap from 200 to 2 cents (100 cents is one semitone).

[**Harmony + melody**](https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/ear-training/?v=4#practice/ensemble) plays a four-chord progression with a melody above it. Rebuild all four chord roots and types, then enter the exact melody pitches on an always-complete C5–B5 chromatic keyboard. Eight levels grow from four to sixteen melody notes, with more keys, chord types, inversions, and chromatic approaches. Note timing is given by the grid. Audition your arrangement or either entered part; empty slots retain their place in time. After grading, compare the original chords and melody separately. A fully correct answer requires both parts; separate chord/note counts are shown and saved in progress. Run `npm run check:ear-training-ensemble` for generator and real browser checks.

Pitch discrimination also offers a **24-question adaptive test** with pure sine tones and one completed listen per trial. Two consecutive correct answers divide the gap by 1.5; a miss multiplies it by 1.5, within 2–200 cents. Feedback is held until the end. The practice estimate is the geometric mean of the last six reversals, requiring at least four; results indicate when the test range was reached or there were too few reversals. Completed tests have a separate history, and unfinished tests are discarded. The result describes a short practice session rather than a calibrated hearing measurement.

Intervals use twelve-tone equal temperament: a minor third is 3 semitones (300 cents), and a major third is 4 (400 cents). Feedback spells the notes to match the named interval, such as C–E♭ for a minor third. After any interval answer, compare minor and major thirds with the same starting pitch, direction, timing, and voice; comparisons do not affect scores. `npm run check:ear-training-thirds` verifies generation, rendered audio frequencies, and the browser comparison controls.

Web Audio synthesizes sounds locally; no microphone, account, samples, or runtime dependencies are needed. First-attempt accuracy, reveals, activity, and completed pitch tests stay in browser storage. Filter practice by module and level, export a JSON backup, or merge one without duplicating questions or tests. Earlier three-level records retain their original labels under legacy filters; saved difficulty preferences move to the expanded scale. Old version-1 backups remain importable.

Editable files live in [`public/ear-training/`](public/ear-training/), with identical published copies in [`docs/ear-training/`](docs/ear-training/). Run `npm run ear-training:sync` after editing; the public copy preserves the subpage through future site builds. `node scripts/sync-ear-training.mjs --check` verifies the copies. Serve `docs/` at port 8765 and run `npm run check:ear-training` with Playwright and Chromium available; `OVERTONE_QA_URL` and `CHROMIUM_PATH` override the browser-check defaults. Checks cover 24,600 seeded questions plus 2,400 dedicated harmony-and-melody cases, unchanged legacy generators, exact fractional pitch frequencies, staircase scoring and bounds, actual browser playback, reconstruction, saved progress, backups, and mobile layouts. The page and module imports include an asset version query so a deployment does not mix cached files from the previous release.

## Clear — cognitive practice

[**Open Clear**](https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/cognitive/) for fourteen mobile-friendly cognitive exercises: dual n-back, spatial span, backward digit span, operation span, symmetry span, Stroop, rule switching, go/no-go attention, visual search, divided visual attention, pattern reasoning, mental rotation, paired recall, and spaced retrieval. Each exercise has clear instructions and scores, with adaptive levels, local saving, and JSON backups. Dual n-back supports spoken letters or offline tones, with separate channel statistics and balanced accuracy. Operation and symmetry span alternate timed processing judgments with items held for ordered recall; recall and processing are scored separately, and both must reach 80% in two consecutive matching sessions to increase difficulty. Spaced retrieval maintains a due-card schedule rather than difficulty levels.

Progress includes eight weeks of activity, same-setting accuracy and correct-response-time charts, latest-five-session comparisons, channel hit/false-alarm rates, complex-span processing accuracy, and expandable session details. Filter by difficulty, n-back sound mode, input/screen settings, and time period; weekly rates use summed counts, and weekly response times use the median of recorded session medians. Existing version-1 scores and review schedules remain compatible; earlier sessions retain their accuracy and show missing measurements as unrecorded. Up to 500 recent sessions are saved locally.

Clear is one self-contained HTML file, with no external assets or runtime dependencies. Download it from the app to practice offline. Its evidence notes cite ten research sources and distinguish practiced-skill gains from unsupported broad intelligence claims; this browser adaptation has not been clinically validated. The editable file is [`public/cognitive/index.html`](public/cognitive/index.html), with an identical published copy at [`docs/cognitive/index.html`](docs/cognitive/index.html). Keep both copies synchronized so the site's normal build preserves it.

For browser checks, serve `docs/` locally and run `node scripts/check-cognitive.cjs`, `node scripts/check-cognitive-upgrade.cjs`, and `node scripts/check-cognitive-progress.cjs` with Playwright available. The default URL is `http://127.0.0.1:8765/cognitive/`; set `CLEAR_QA_URL` to override it and `CHROMIUM_PATH` to choose a browser. The checks cover actual n-back and complex-span scoring, processing-gated difficulty, saved metrics, history compatibility, settings filters, weekly rate aggregation, missing response times, backup handling, background interruption, small-screen layouts, and the downloaded standalone file.

### Mental math

Search the catalog, filter by topic or tag, save favorites, or build a custom mix. Five suggested routes cover gentle arithmetic, algebra, calculus, professor-level challenges, and interactive visual practice.

Practice is untimed, sprints run for 60/90/180 seconds, and daily sets are reproducible for the same configuration. Choose typed numerical answers or multiple choice; symbolic questions use choices. A safe numerical parser supports fractions, parentheses, powers, π/e and numerical functions without evaluating JavaScript. Every question includes a strategy hint and a worked solution. Hints mark answers as assisted, reveals/skips count as misses, and only unassisted sessions adjust difficulty. Daily replays keep the original daily result and cannot change difficulty.

Math scores integrate with Clear’s same-setting progress charts. Favorites, session settings, daily records and existing cognitive scores remain compatible with version-1 backups. The app remains one self-contained HTML file with no external scripts or assets. Run `node scripts/check-cognitive-math.cjs` for seeded question checks, real typed/choice/visual sessions, assistance rules, daily determinism, sprint deadlines, saved math data, backups and mobile controls. Generator answers were additionally checked with independent arithmetic, algebraic, combinatorial, symbolic and numerical methods during development.

## Second Pour

[**Play Second Pour**](https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/second-pour/) from behind the bar of a softly illustrated neighborhood café, with playful DynaPuff headings and rounded Nunito lettering. Every visible display and control is drawn inside the game canvas, including orders, recipes, conversations, machines, and shift results. Place your cup under the espresso spout, pull the lever for one shot, then move the filled cup back to the tray or another station. The cup stays visibly under the machine while it fills. Dropping onto the espresso start button places and starts it together. Drag your cup to milk or kettle to fill it, and slide pastries into the warmer before moving warm food to the tray. Drop a finished cup or tray food onto a guest or Serve. On phones, you can also tap an item and its destination; Cancel puts it down. Practice with instant machines, then take on three-minute shifts, matching recipes and chatting with the regulars to earn tips. Later days add new drinks and continue their stories; your best shifts are saved in the browser. Mouse and touch dragging, tap controls, and keyboard navigation are supported.

Editable React/Vite sources are in [`second-pour/`](second-pour/). Install its dependencies with `npm --prefix second-pour ci`, then run `npm run second-pour:build` from the repository root to rebuild and refresh both `public/second-pour/` and the published `docs/second-pour/`. Run this command after editing the game so the parent site's regular build includes the latest version.

Run `npm --prefix second-pour test` for game and pointer rules, and `npm --prefix second-pour run test:e2e` for the production browser regression suite. See [`second-pour/tests/README.md`](second-pour/tests/README.md) for browser coverage and input limitations.

On phones and smaller screens, station tabs show one roomy machine at a time while the order, cup, food tray, and Serve button stay visible. Tap a cup or pastry and then its destination, or drag it; tap warmed food to collect. Touch taps tolerate small finger movements, and the drag preview stays above your finger.

## Download and play

Download [**sidequest-prototypes.zip**](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/calebhaines/sidequest-prototypes/v0.6.0/docs/sidequest-prototypes.zip), extract it, and open **sidequest/PLAY.html** in your browser. For a single file, save [**sidequest-play.html**](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/calebhaines/sidequest-prototypes/v0.6.0/docs/sidequest-play.html) and open the saved HTML. These downloads are fixed to **v0.6.0**.

All eight versions, artwork, 3D code, previews, and fonts are bundled for offline play. No account, installation, or server is needed. The 3D versions require a browser with WebGL enabled. Three.js is included locally; playing does not download libraries or models from other sites.

## The two 3D finalists

[**Velvet Static 3D**](https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/?v=0.6.0#play-lynch-3d) turns its Lynch-inspired county mystery into a world of shaded buildings, solid characters, pine forests, a red-curtain theater, paper mill, observatory, railway, and ferry. Cross between waking and dreaming to investigate contradictory accounts.

[**Hidden Ember 3D**](https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/?v=0.6.0#play-shinobi-3d) brings its original Naruto-inspired shinobi adventure into orange-roofed villages, cedar forests, training grounds, clan outposts, and waterfall country. Use kunai, chakra techniques, conversations, and competing loyalties to pursue the stolen scroll.

Both use real 3D geometry and a perspective camera with orbit and zoom. Their existing quests, conversations, remembered choices, real-time combat, loot, levels, side stories, travel stops, and three endings carry over through the shared gameplay engine.

Each finalist has a **4800 × 3456 simulation map**, **14 characters**, **six main objectives**, **three side stories**, and **six travel stops**. All enclosed buildings are enterable in 2D and 3D: **31 interiors** in Velvet Static and **25** in Hidden Ember. Explore furnished rooms, inspect objects, and find residents and clues inside. Velvet Static's dream crossings extend indoors.

These are playable concept adventures for developing a larger RPG. Gameplay starts fresh when you reopen a version.

## Controls

**WASD / arrows** move; **Shift** sprints; **E / Enter** talks, inspects, enters doors, or leaves through an indoor exit; **Space** attacks; **M** opens the map; **T** opens discovered travel stops in the finalists; **Escape** closes dialogue or the map, or returns to the gallery. Touch controls and clickable abilities are available. Ambient sound is optional.

In 3D, **drag** the game view to orbit and **scroll** to zoom. On-screen camera buttons also rotate, zoom, and reset the view. Movement follows the current camera direction; reset restores the initial north-facing view.

**Velvet Static:** Q crosses between waking and dream, including indoors; Space flashes the camera. **Hidden Ember:** 1 Ember Release, 2 Shadow Clone, 3 Substitution; Space throws kunai. Chakra regenerates. Ask Sora about the watchtower passphrase for a peaceful route.

Use E at a marked doorstep to enter, and E at the indoor exit to leave. **M** shows the outdoor map with your location at the doorway; **Room floor plan** shows the furnished room and exit. **T** can travel from a room to a discovered outdoor stop. Accept side stories in conversations and track them from the sidebar. Progress and dialogue choices carry through entrances, exits, dream crossings, and travel during an adventure.
