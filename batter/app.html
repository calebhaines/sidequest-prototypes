BATTER / OPERATING NOTES

Beat the mixture senseless.

Start with a good recipe.

Press Play: a complete recorded acoustic kit is already loaded. Choose a kit or house recipe for a fresh starting point. Click a square to add or remove a hit; Shift-click selects it without changing it. The left drum pads audition each lane. Mute and Solo are beside every pad.

A–H are independent pattern banks. Clear pattern removes all 64 stored hits in every lane of the selected bank, including hidden and protected hits, and clears any received performance. It keeps other banks and every sound setting; Undo restores it. Copy bank copies the current recipe to another bank. Chain plays the bank letters in order. Patterns can have 16, 32, or 64 steps; the arrows show the next page of 16.

Make the clock misbehave.

Each hit has velocity, chance, offset, repeat rolls, flams, conditional playback, and pitch. Parameter locks change selected sound settings for one hit. Protect a hit or lane to spare it from recipe tools. Euclidean distributes a chosen number of hits through a lane; Mutate makes small seeded changes; Make a fill puts more movement at the end.

Independent lane lengths allow different loops to meet at different points. Swing delays alternate steps. Humanize gently changes timing and velocity; it is separate from deliberate per-hit offsets.

Prepare the ingredients.

Select a lane to see its sample waveform. Drag either edge to trim, or adjust the precise Start and End controls. Pitch, level, pan, reverse, normalization, envelope, choke groups, filtering, distortion and bit reduction shape the recorded drum. Choke groups cut off earlier hits, useful for open and closed hi-hats.

Change source A or Choose source B opens the drum cupboard, with search, categories, collection credits, and audition. Bring your own audio files or Kitchen sample bundles. Larger optional libraries are loaded on request; the starter kit stays playable offline.

Mix and stir.

The mixing station combines two real samples per lane. Choose B, then use the equal-power A/B fader: the ends play one source; the middle combines both. Source B has independent pitch, fine tuning, level, arrival delay, reverse and recorded-sample variation. The lane trim, envelope, filters and texture shape both sources. The small A and B play buttons audition each ingredient alone.

The automatic whisk is one LFO per lane. Sine, triangle, square, saw and seeded random shapes can move pitch, cutoff, pan, level or the A/B balance. Choose a free Hz rate or a tempo division. Hit reset restarts the chosen phase for each strike; Continuous follows the song clock. Blend modulation needs a chosen B and Blend on.

Keep and share.

Save downloads the complete BATTER project, including imported audio. Open restores it. Export WAV renders the pattern or chain with its tails. Export pattern and Export samples create portable Kitchen exchanges. Samples and Patterns in the header share with other Kitchen instruments and GALLEY when your browser storage is available.

Space plays / stops. Escape silences all drums. Ctrl / Command Z undoes; Shift Z redoes. Number keys 1–9, then 0, minus and equals audition the twelve lanes. Drum notes received over GALLEY's host connection can play these same samples.

Autosave keeps the complete recipe and imported audio on this device when browser storage is available. The footer confirms when saving finishes. Download Save for a portable backup. Audio starts after a click. Device, browser and interface settings determine playback latency.
