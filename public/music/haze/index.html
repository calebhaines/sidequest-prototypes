EXTRACTION SYSTEM / OPERATING NOTES

The hood has
instructions.

STEAM turns a drawing into an additive score. Each horizontal band is a pitched tone; each column is a moment in a tempo-linked loop. Brightness sets amplitude. Every band uses a fundamental and a quieter coloured overtone.

  1. Press Play, then brush across the score. Use Erase to remove cells.
  2. Choose a root and pitch map. Stretch changes the spacing of notes; Colour adds an overtone. Blur blends nearby cells; Drift gently detunes the oscillators.
  3. Freeze holds the current column. Select a cell and use Scan here to hear that point. Forward, Reverse, and Ping-pong choose the reading direction.
  4. Capture records the live stereo output, up to three minutes. Press it again to download. Export WAV renders a fresh pass from your saved scan position, with three seconds of tail.
  5. Save / Open keeps the entire score and controls. Your latest project also stays in this browser when storage is available.

Space Play / Stop · Esc Panic · Ctrl / ⌘ Z Undo
On the canvas: Arrows choose a cell, Enter paints, Delete erases. The precise cell editor is also available for keyboard and touch.

Stop releases the voices and keeps effect tails. Panic silences everything and discards a live capture. STEAM never needs a microphone, samples, or an internet connection.
