STATION BRIEFING / IMPACT & RESONANCE

Conduct the cookware.

A mallet and a burst of noise put energy into a vibrating body. Its resonances turn that impact into a drum, bell, or string. The kitchen calls this a perfectly reasonable use of a saucepan.

Build your beat

Click a step to add a hit. Shift + click for an accent, or press and hold a step on a touchscreen. Select a voice to shape it. M mutes a track; S hears it solo. Pattern banks A–D hold four variations.

Find your body

Choose one of nine resonators, including a bell, a singing bowl, and a tube. Tune its pitch, structure, decay, damping, and strike position. Motion + mix adds movement and places the voice in the stereo field.

Choose your contact

Exciter offers felt, rubber, wood, nylon, ceramic, and metal alongside the original neutral mallet. Each shapes the contact differently. Refine hardness and contact time, add surface texture, or let the mallet rebound. Noise has its own color, filter, and envelope. Drag knobs vertically; hold Shift for fine adjustments.

Keep a good idea

Your changes save in this browser automatically. Save project downloads an editable JSON file; the folder opens it again. Export WAV renders four bars with a natural decay tail as a stereo audio file.
