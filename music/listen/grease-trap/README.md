# Grease Trap

An original industrial funk instrumental composed with Kitchen and arranged,
mixed, automated, saved, and exported in GALLEY 1.11.1. Duration: 3:01.
Key: D minor, with E-flat and A-flat tension. Musical pulse: 106 BPM.

## Open the session

1. Unzip `Grease-Trap-GALLEY-project.zip`.
2. Open GALLEY at https://calebhaines.github.io/sidequest-prototypes/music/loom/,
   or use the current downloaded standalone GALLEY HTML.
3. Click **Open**, then choose `Grease-Trap.loom.json`.
4. Return to the start and press **Play**. Loop and metronome are off.

All 25 source recordings are embedded in the project. The arrangement, clip
edits, eight tracks, four effect slots per track, markers, and automation remain
editable. The instrument states are saved with their respective tracks.

GALLEY uses a **53 BPM half-time grid** for this session: one GALLEY bar contains
two 106 BPM pulse bars. Forty GALLEY bars cover the full 80-bar performance.
Keep the project tempo at 53 to preserve the recorded arrangement.

## Source material

The performances are printed Kitchen audio, with original source patches and
portable note patterns in `Sources/`. Load a native source project in its
respective instrument to edit the sound, or import a pattern through the shared
Patterns dialog. Source performances use 106 BPM and four pulse bars.

SIZZLE supplies low drums and relay hats; CLATTER supplies tight snares and
ceramic strikes; ROUX supplies the sliding bass; LEAVEN supplies the muted synth
hook; REDUCE supplies metal punctuation; MARINADE supplies vowel ghosts; STEAM
and ROTISSERIE supply the disturbed room. GALLEY's SVT-inspired BROILER bass
model adds protected lows and a changing amount of grit.

The separate `Grease-Trap.wav` is the native GALLEY stereo export: 48 kHz,
16-bit PCM, with no appended tail or processing after export. The composition,
patterns and sounds are original; no third-party recordings were used.
