SKEWER / OPERATING NOTES

Four strings. A slightly bent service.

Four physical string lanes run independent patterns. Press Play for the sequencer, or pluck notes with the keyboard. Select a lane to shape its string, rhythm and mix.

Set the pattern

Each row has 16 steps. Length decides how many are used; each lane has its own division and direction. Click a step to toggle it and select its editor. Shift-click or right-click selects without changing it. Scale degree, Velocity and Probability belong to each step. Root and Scale tune the whole instrument.

Shape the strings

Decay sets the loop's loss; Brightness controls high frequency damping. Pluck position changes which harmonics the excitation favours. Finger, Pick, Hammer and Wire have different noise transients. Tension detunes the string in cents. Dispersion adds stiff-string inharmonicity; strong settings can shift pitch. Body adds a broad wooden, metallic, glassy or hollow resonance. String and body changes apply to new plucks; Level, Pan, Mute and Solo respond while notes ring.

Set independent clocks

Euclidean rhythm distributes pulses through the current length. Scatter notes changes active notes within the scale. Swing alternates long and short step intervals; Humanize adds up to 45 ms of delayed timing and slight velocity variation. Seed makes probability and evolution reproducible when restarting or exporting. Evolution nudges played notes without rewriting the score.

Keep the order

Save score downloads a complete SKEWER project. Open accepts SKEWER projects, including earlier BOWER files. Undo restores score edits. Capture mix records live stereo sound for up to 3 minutes. Export WAV renders 1–16 bars at 48 kHz with an 8-second tail. Stop halts new sequenced notes while strings ring out. Panic immediately clears voices, effects and any unfinished capture.

Useful keys

Space: Play / Stop. A S D F G H J K: pluck scale notes. 1–4: select lane. Esc: Panic. Ctrl / Command Z: Undo. Typing into controls leaves shortcuts alone. Hold Damp to shorten ringing strings; Strum plays all four lanes.

SKEWER works entirely in your browser. It requests no microphone access. Audio starts after a click or key press. Your score is saved locally when storage is available; downloadable projects work even when storage is blocked.
